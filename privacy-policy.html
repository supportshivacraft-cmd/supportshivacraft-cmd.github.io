1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in with your Google account via Android Credential Manager / Firebase Authentication, we receive your public profile details (such as your display name, email address, and profile picture URL) provided by Google.
  • Game & Progress Data: Your gameplay statistics, including completed levels, high scores, coin balances, hint unlocks, daily challenge streaks, and bookmarked vocabulary definitions.
  • User Support Communications: Any feedback, bug reports, or customer service queries you send to us via email.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Technical Identifiers: Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, language settings, and IP address.
  • Android Advertising Identifier (AAID): The Google Play Services Advertising ID assigned to your device. This identifier is accessed in accordance with Google Play developer policies and permissions (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) to serve, frequency-cap, and measure personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
  • Analytics and Ad Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, rewarded video completions, clicks, and diagnostic telemetry used to combat fraud and monitor app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Gameplay & Cloud Sync: To store your player profile, vocabulary milestones, and high scores securely in the cloud using Google Cloud Firestore so that your progress is never lost when changing devices.
  • Advertising Display & Monetization: To display interstitial ads and rewarded video advertisements (which grant in-game coins, hints, or puzzle unlocks) through Google AdMob.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: To detect bot activity, prevent abuse of in-game rewards, and verify the integrity of the application.
  • App Performance & Improvements: To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, optimize loading speeds, and balance puzzle difficulty for competitive examinations (UPSC, SSC, Banking).

3. Advertising & Android Advertising ID (AAID)

Word Power is supported in part by advertising provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ad Formats: The App serves interstitial advertisements between puzzle sets and optional rewarded video ads that players can choose to watch to earn in-game rewards (e.g., bonus coins, hints).
  • Android Advertising ID: AdMob utilizes your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID) and technical data to deliver, measure, and attribute advertisements. Depending on your device settings and jurisdiction, ads may be personalized (tailored to your interests) or non-personalized (contextual).
  • How to Reset or Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized advertising at any time directly through your Android device settings:
    1. Open your device's Settings app.
    2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
    3. Tap Reset advertising ID to generate a fresh identifier, or tap Delete advertising ID to remove access for advertising personalization.
